Your Koht in town

Koht sits on Lai 8, in the middle of Tallinn's Old Town, doing double duty as cafe by day and bar by night.

Coffee and something to eat when you need to sit down for a while. Drinks and a slower pace once the evening starts. Either way, it's built for people who just want a table and no fuss.
