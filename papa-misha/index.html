The kitchen

Burgers before the beach

Papa Misha sits on Albatrosu iela, a short walk from the Mangaļsala pier. It's a takeaway counter with a small, quiet terrace for eating in.

The menu is built around smash burgers made with Latvian beef. There are salads for something lighter, and Rocketbean coffee to go with either.

  • Smash burgers made with Latvian beef
  • Small outdoor terrace
  • Rocketbean coffee served
