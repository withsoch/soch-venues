The kitchen

Your local, every day

Baras Justiniškės sits by PC Norfa, in the middle of the Justiniškės residential district in Vilnius.

There is a small kitchen behind the bar, so food comes alongside the drinks rather than instead of them. Lunch and bar snacks are on offer next to the usual beer and wine.

  • Beer bar with a small kitchen
  • Lunch and bar snacks served daily
  • Banquet hall on site

Simple food to go with a simple pint

Lunch, snacks
and a cold one

The kitchen keeps it straightforward: lunch dishes and bar snacks made to order, meant to be eaten with a beer or a glass of wine.
