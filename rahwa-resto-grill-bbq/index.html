The kitchen

Lunch, done plainly

Rahwa Resto Grill & BBQ is a self-service canteen inside Sepa Keskus in Tartu, built for a quick, filling midday meal.

The daily menu runs through Estonian home cooking: soups, cabbage rolls, schnitzel and cutlets, plus grilled meats from the BBQ side. Nothing fussy, just food that comes out fast and doesn't cost much.

  • Daily päevapraad menu
  • Most dishes under €7
  • Free parking at Sepa Keskus

Grilled meats alongside the everyday Estonian lunch classics

Off the grill

Alongside the soups and cutlets, the grill turns out barbecued meats for anyone wanting something more substantial with their lunch.
