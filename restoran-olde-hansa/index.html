The kitchen

A taste of old Tallinn

Restoran Olde Hansa sits on Vana turg, right by Tallinn's Town Hall Square, in a building that leans fully into the city's medieval past.

The menu draws on old recipes and ingredients that would have been familiar centuries ago. Expect hearty, slow-cooked dishes served in a room lit by candles and furnished to match.

  • Medieval-themed dining room
  • Located on Tallinn's Vana turg
  • Open all seven days

Dishes built around old recipes and traditions

Food from another century

The kitchen leans on game, root vegetables, bread and slow-cooked meats, the kind of food that suited candlelight and long tables.
