The kitchen

Pizza, Buenos Aires style

Q Pizza&Pan sits on the Depoo food street inside Telliskivi Creative City, a quick stop for wood-fired pizza with an Argentinean accent.

Chef Pablo Zabala bakes his pizzas in a Buenos Aires-style wood-fired oven, blending Italian technique with Argentinean habits. The menu runs from a classic Margherita to pizza-burgers, built for people who want something good fast.

  • Buenos Aires-style wood-fired oven
  • Argentinean-style pizza, Italian spirit
  • Dine-in, outdoor seating and delivery
