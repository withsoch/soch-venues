The kitchen

Kebab, pizza,
no sitting around

Tallinn Pizza & Kebab is a counter-service takeaway on Aia tn, built for quick orders and late nights.

The menu runs through doner kebab in pita, wrap or lavash, alongside pizzas, shawarma and grilled chicken plates. A few Turkish sweets round things off for anyone after something after the meal.

  • Doner kebab in pita, wrap or lavash
  • Pizza, shawarma and grilled chicken
  • Open roughly 10am to 6am

The order that gets picked most at the counter

Doner, your way

Kebab meat comes stuffed into pita, wrapped or rolled in lavash, whichever suits the walk or the ride home.
